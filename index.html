Help & app guide
How do I unlock Halo?

Solve a challenge, select Copy key, then paste it into Halo and select Unlock. Your browser and the app remember your key until its original expiry. Refreshing never extends it.

After expiry, solve a new challenge. Use File → Forget saved key in Halo to clear saved access.

Puzzles, math, and attempts

For Lights Out, turn every tile off. Reset restores the same board; New challenge starts another. Use arrow keys to move and Space to flip a tile.

Math ranges from pre-algebra through calculus. Exact fractions and decimals are accepted. Each challenge lasts 15 minutes and allows five failed attempts. Your key lasts 24 hours from a successful solution.

Writing and running scripts

New or + creates a tab. Open can load several files. Save, Clear, and Execute use the selected tab; Execute becomes Stop while a script runs. Output shows results and clickable errors.

Use ⌘F to find text. Library keeps saved scripts and favorites; Settings controls the editor. Type part of a name, then press Tab to accept a suggestion or Escape to dismiss it.

Halo runs Luau locally. Roblox APIs such as game and workspace are unavailable.

local message = "Hello, Halo"
print(message)

Copying or access trouble?

If automatic copying is blocked, open View full key, select the complete key, and press ⌘C or Ctrl+C. Paste it into Halo 2.7 or later.

Keep your device clock correct. Keys are signed by the Halo key service; old demo keys are retired. If a challenge expires or locks, select New challenge.
