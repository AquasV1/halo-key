GOOD TO KNOW

A few quick answers.

Will I need to enter the key every time?

Halo remembers a valid key until its original 24-hour expiry. Closing the app, refreshing this page, or entering the key again does not reset that deadline. After expiry, solve a new challenge. File → Forget saved key clears the saved key on your device.

How do code suggestions work?

Pause after typing part of a word, or a known library such as math. A faint ending appears only when there is enough empty space at the end of the line. Press Tab to accept or Escape to dismiss. Enter makes a new line. Use Option + Escape on Mac or Ctrl + Space on Windows/Linux to request or cycle suggestions.

What scripts can Halo run?

Halo runs standard Luau locally in its own runtime. Try print, tables, functions, and math. Roblox game APIs such as game, workspace, and Instance are not available in this local workspace.

How does the 24-hour expiry work?

The countdown begins when your correct answer generates a key, not when you first paste it into Halo. The website and desktop app check the same deadline. The service validates your answer and signs the original issue and expiry times. Halo verifies that signature using a public key. Browser code cannot issue new signed keys.

How do the light puzzles work?

Flip a tile to toggle it and its horizontal and vertical neighbors. Your goal is an entirely dark board. Every generated board is solvable, and each challenge stays open for 15 minutes. Use Reset board to start the same puzzle again, or New challenge for a different board. Keyboard users can move with arrow keys and press Space to flip a tile.

What kinds of math problems will I get?

Choose a level or let Halo surprise you: multi-step arithmetic, linear equations and systems, quadratics and exponentials, function composition and series, or derivatives and definite integrals. Answers can be exact fractions or decimals. An incorrect answer never reveals a key.

The copy button isn’t working. What can I do?

After solving, select the full generated key above, then press ⌘C on Mac or Ctrl+C on Windows/Linux. Return to Halo, paste it into the key field, and select Unlock.
